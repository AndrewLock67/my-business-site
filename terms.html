2. Our service

JAO provides job-search and application support. Depending on your package, this includes finding job openings that match the preferences you give us, sending you the roles we find for your approval, preparing and customizing your resume and other application materials, submitting applications to the roles you approve, and tracking their progress in an Application Tracker that we send you when your order is complete.

JAO is not an employer, recruiter or staffing agency, and we don't make or influence hiring decisions. Each package covers the number of applications you buy, as described on our Services & Pricing page.

We propose openings based on your preferences and the openings available, and you decide which ones we apply to. We may skip openings we can't apply to properly, for example because a job site doesn't allow applications submitted on someone's behalf.

Timing. We usually start finding roles within 5 business days of receiving your complete details. Once you've approved all your roles, we aim to complete your applications within 1–2 weeks, depending on our current volume of work. Time spent waiting for your approval doesn't count toward this estimate. These are estimates, not guarantees.

4. Applying on your behalf

When you place an order and complete onboarding, you authorize JAO to search for openings that match your preferences and to send them to you for approval. We only submit applications to roles you approve. Approving a role authorizes us to prepare application materials and submit an application for you to that role, within the number of applications you bought.

If you decline a proposed role, we'll look for a replacement, for up to 2 rounds of replacements. Applications that still don't have an approved role after that are refunded under our Refund & Cancellation Policy. If you don't approve any of the roles we propose, we'll refund your order in full.

Your job-search email. We apply using a dedicated Gmail address that you create and own, and you set it to forward a copy of incoming mail to JAO. This lets us complete verification steps and track responses without ever needing your password. Any job-site accounts we create for you use this address, so you keep control of them. You can turn off forwarding at any time, but doing so before your order is complete may delay the remaining applications. You remain responsible for any accounts in your name and for following the terms of the job sites you use.

You can change your preferences or stop further applications at any time by contacting us. Applications already submitted can't be withdrawn by us; you may need to contact the employer directly.
