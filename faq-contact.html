What does Job Application Office (JAO) do?

JAO helps you manage your job search from start to finish. We find suitable job openings, send them to you for approval, personalize your resume and application materials, submit applications to the roles you approve, and track your progress.

How does the process work?
  1. You choose a package, complete your purchase, and share your resume, experience, preferred roles and locations through our short onboarding form.
  2. Our team reviews your details and finds suitable openings for you.
  3. We email you the list of roles to approve.
  4. Once you've approved your roles, we tailor your materials and submit your applications, logging each one in your Application Tracker.
  5. When your order is complete, we confirm it and send you your Application Tracker.
How long does it take?

We usually start finding your roles within 5 business days of receiving your complete details.

Once you've approved all your roles, we aim to complete your applications within 1–2 weeks. Timing can vary with our current volume of work. Time spent waiting for your approval doesn't count toward this, so the sooner you approve, the sooner we apply.

How do you find jobs that fit my needs?

We use the preferences you provide to guide our search. These include your desired roles, industries, locations, qualifications, and other job criteria. Providing clear, accurate information helps us identify relevant opportunities.

What is included in the service?

Your service includes finding job openings that match your goals, a list of roles for you to approve by email, resume and application-material customization, submission of every application you approve, and an Application Tracker showing each application we've made for you.

How much does JAO cost?

Pricing depends on the number of applications you purchase.

  • 1–10 applications: $5.00 per application.
  • 11–25 applications: $4.75 per application.
  • 26–75 applications: $4.50 per application.
  • 76+ applications: $4.25 per application.

Qualifying discounts apply to every application in your order. You can review your total before completing checkout. Calculate your total.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes. You can cancel all or part of your order at any time by emailing support@jobappoffice.com.

  • Unused applications are fully refundable for 6 months after purchase.
  • Applications we've already started or submitted generally aren't refundable.
  • If you cancel part of a package, the applications you used are re-priced at the package rate for that quantity.
  • If we can't complete your order because suitable openings run out, we refund every incomplete application in full, at any time.

See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for full details.

Do I approve each job before you apply?

Yes, every one. Nothing is sent without your OK, so you stay in control of where your name goes.

  • We email you the roles we've found that match your goals.
  • Approve the ones you want, and we start applying.
  • Turn any down, and we'll find replacements, for up to 2 rounds. Any applications still without an approved role after that are refunded at the price you paid.
  • If you don't approve any of the roles we propose, we'll refund your order in full.
Do you need my passwords or log in as me?

Some job sites require an account to apply. When that happens, we'll let you know and agree with you how it will be handled before we go ahead. You stay in control of any accounts in your name.

Do you use AI?

JAO is proud to be a human-driven company. Every application is prepared by people, for people. We may use AI tools to help proofread and draft materials, but our team does the work. We only share what's needed for the task, and we never enter sensitive information into AI tools.

Are interviews or job offers guaranteed?

No. JAO helps you manage your applications, but employers make their own hiring decisions. Outcomes depend on factors such as your qualifications, employer requirements, and market conditions.

What information do I need to provide?

You will need to provide your resume, education, work experience, preferred roles, industries, locations, and other relevant job criteria. We may request additional information needed to prepare individual applications.
