Frequently Asked Questions

What does EZApps do?

EZApps helps you manage your job search from start to finish. We identify suitable job openings, personalize your resume and application materials, submit applications with your authorization, and track your progress.

How does the process work?

First, you choose an application package and complete your purchase. Next, you share your resume, experience, job preferences, and other relevant details through onboarding. Once we have the information and authorization we need, we begin identifying suitable roles and preparing your applications.

How long does it take?

Timing depends on the information you provide, the availability of suitable openings, and the requirements of each application. We can begin once your onboarding is complete and the necessary details are available. Contact us if you have questions about your order's progress.

How do you find jobs that fit my needs?

We use the preferences you provide to guide our search. These include your desired roles, industries, locations, qualifications, and other job criteria. Providing clear, accurate information helps us identify relevant opportunities.

What is included in the service?

Your service includes job opportunity discovery, resume and application-material customization, application submissions with your authorization, and application progress tracking.

How much does EZApps cost?

Pricing depends on the number of applications you purchase.

  • 1–9 applications: $5.00 per application.
  • 10–24 applications: $4.75 per application.
  • 25–78 applications: $4.50 per application.
  • 79 or more applications: $4.25 per application.

Qualifying discounts apply to every application in your order. You can review your total before completing checkout. Calculate your total.

Are interviews or job offers guaranteed?

No. EZApps helps you manage your applications, but employers make their own hiring decisions. Outcomes depend on factors such as your qualifications, employer requirements, and market conditions.

What information do I need to provide?

You will need to provide your resume, education, work experience, preferred roles, industries, locations, and other relevant job criteria. We may request additional information needed to prepare individual applications.

How do I contact EZApps about my order?

Use the contact form below to send us your question. Include your email address and a clear description of your request so our team can understand how to help.
