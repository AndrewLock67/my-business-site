How your total is calculated

Number of applications × the price per application for your package. For example:

  • 5 applications × $5.00 = $25.00
  • 11 applications × $4.75 = $52.25
  • 26 applications × $4.50 = $117.00
  • 76 applications × $4.25 = $323.00

These examples show package totals before any additional charges, if applicable. Your checkout should display the final amount and applicable discount before you pay.

What You Need to Provide

To help us find suitable opportunities, please provide accurate and up-to-date information, including:

  • Your current resume.
  • Your education and work experience.
  • Your preferred roles and industries.
  • Your preferred locations and other job criteria.
  • Any additional information needed for your applications.

Complete onboarding helps us understand your goals and identify relevant opportunities.
